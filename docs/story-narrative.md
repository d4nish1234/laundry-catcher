# Laundry Catchers: Journey to Umrah — Narrative Guide

This is the writing reference for the visual-novel journey and the magical laundry-card thread that connects its hotel chapters.

## Theme and tone

A Canadian Muslim family performs Umrah. Through Hamza, Enayah, Isa, and their family, children experience the spiritual journey from Madinah to Makkah while learning about Islamic rituals, holy places, hospitality, and Muslim unity.

The tone is warm, funny, and awe-filled. Teaching happens naturally through wonder and conversation, never through a lecture.

## Laundry-card story

- A hotel attendant or an unexpected discovery introduces each laundry card.
- Turning over a card reveals a real garment or travel item.
- The picture slips into an abstract sea made from rolling fabric folds.
- The card's clothespin charm is cast on a rope to look for the pictured item.
- A pull can return the pictured item, a different local item, or nothing.
- Found items become factual collection cards.
- The cards are mysterious journey keepsakes, not creatures, opponents, or battle tools.

## Writing rules

1. Keep dialogue short and focused on one idea at a time.
2. Let characters react emotionally before another character explains a fact.
3. Use narration for atmosphere, transitions, and moments of wonder.
4. Introduce Arabic terms with an immediate child-friendly explanation.
5. Include the salutation ﷺ when mentioning the Prophet ﷺ by title or name.
6. End scenes on warmth, energy, humor, or wonder.
7. Avoid monster-catching slogans, creature language, battle language, rarity language, and promises to collect everything.
8. Prefer commas, periods, colons, and ellipses over em dashes in on-screen dialogue.

## Core educational arc

- Arrival and Madinah: what Umrah is, the five daily prayers, Masjid Al-Nabawi, and Muslim unity.
- Preparation and travel: Ihram, Miqat, Talbiyyah, and the road to Makkah.
- Makkah: first sight of the Kaabah, Tawaf, prayer, and Zamzam.
- Completion: Sa'i between Safa and Marwah, the story of Hajar, and cutting the hair.
- Homeward journey: gratitude, family memories, and hope to return.
